# Design direction for the results lab (2026-11-07)

Yash pointed to [unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir), CC BY 4.0) as the standard for spacing and craft. Its method is applied here by hand; the skill itself is not installed. Its core rule: quiet chrome, and the richness goes into one or two moments of content.

- **Product.** An experiment on Qwen3-4B that runs in front of the visitor: how it is served more cheaply and how it was trained to be smarter, with every number read from the lab's model.
- **Character.** Exact, unhurried, a little theatrical.
- **World.** An instrument bench at night: traces on a time axis, a lab notebook, a highlighter.
- **Moments.** The moving 3D graph of the 96 requests, the replay's converging clusters, the inside-the-model relief, the capability terrain.
- **Signature.** The highlighter: one stroke wipes across the one phrase that matters, in the colour of its measure, then rests. It is used nowhere else and never loops.

## Rules taken from it, for this lab
- One neutral ramp (ink) and three text roles. One accent per branch. Blue, green, violet and coral keep their meanings only: baseline, optimized, Δ, problem.
- No glows, coloured shadows, gradient text, shimmer, looping pulses, italic emphasis or tracked capitals. Depth comes from surface steps and hairlines.
- Spacing on the 4 px scale, with less space inside a group than between groups. Navigation rows 32–36 tall, 2–4 apart.
- Weights 400 and 500. Letter spacing 0 under 16 px; tightening only on display sizes. Tabular figures.
- Short strings have no trailing period. A change says what it compares to.
- One easing curve, `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`: 120–180 ms for feedback, 300–500 ms for entrances, rise 6–12 px. Colour, opacity and transform only.
- One main chart on a screen; anything else that shows data is smaller and different in kind.

## Where this differs from what Yash first suggested
Yash suggested "sparkles or stars, or some very nice way" to highlight one phrase. Sparkles, shimmer and gradient text are the first things unslop lists as AI tells, so the highlight is a highlighter stroke instead. The blur that opens the lab stays, because Yash asked for it and it carries meaning: it directs attention to one thing.
