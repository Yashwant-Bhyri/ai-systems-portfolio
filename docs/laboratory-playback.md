## Continuous film / practical reveal edition

The viewer-facing checkpoint is: **see the work happen, understand what it changes for a request, then inspect the cost and outcome**. Motion guidance informs the material and accessibility; the causal storyboard determines what actually moves.

- Inference chapters last 12 seconds. Roughly the first 60% plays the mechanism to its conclusion; a short hold precedes the dissolve into a concrete contract-comparison request. The lenses remain manually selectable.
- The scheduling film fills actual service bars against a shared wall clock. Request states, completions and worked GPU-seconds come from scenario timestamps, not a decorative moving cursor.
- The prefix film shows cold computation, cache readiness, consolidation, and later matching requests. These conceptual later requests are explicitly not the first three arrivals in the numerical cohort. Unrelated-context mode cannot claim a reuse hit.
- The execution film computes visible output progress from the selected task's actual simulated prefill/decode times. Both runtimes retain the same output-token count.
- The composition film separates queueing, avoided input work and per-token rates. Scheduling alone leaves busy GPU-seconds unchanged.
- SFT and relative-reward films dissolve from the mathematics into a visibly checked discount-plus-shipping request. Policy updates remain offline; a live request uses the resulting checkpoint.
- LoRA motion interpolates A and B and recomputes BA and the forward pass at every frame. Intermediate frames are animation between optimizer steps, not additional training steps.
- Test-time scaling gets a full-width, two-lens scene: actual-cost cohort curves alongside a repository assistant investigating duplicate payment retries. The practical side draws the lost response and the difference between reusing an idempotency key and creating another charge. It is a teaching scenario, not a captured run or one of the plotted task records.
- Its 21-second sequence moves through 1×, 2×, 4× and 8×, then returns to the selected comparison allowance. The next efficiency-policy chapter therefore retains that allowance. Evidence inspection follows the currently shown point, including during the budget sweep.
- The extra-thought curve is not an invented exponential. Four declared simulator knots are connected; the moving cursor illustrates their transition, not an extra measurement.

The same active-viewing clock drives chapter advancement, the visible playhead and scrubbing. No second animation clock drifts away from the chapter timer. Each scene has a nearby play control. Outgoing scenes remain briefly mounted and inert for a dissolve, with their clocks stopped. No new animation dependency was added.

## Component narratives

Each mechanism is designed independently, then connected through the same request identities and explicit evaluation boundary.

| Component | Mathematical or systems event | Meaning for a request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SFT / LoRA | Frozen W; trainable rank-2 factors A and B; exact product and gradient steps | Learn a complete sequence of operations instead of skipping a condition |
| Relative-reward learning | Four attempts, verifier rewards, group-standardized advantages, normalized policy tilt | Favor checked solutions over fluent but incomplete ones |
| Test-time search | Fixed weights; progressively expand and verify candidates | Recover an answer missed by the first attempt, while paying for failures |
| Efficiency-aware learning | A declared cost penalty distinguishes two correct trajectories | Prefer a direct valid route without removing necessary checks |
| Scheduling | Release a worker without waiting for a paired request | Reduce waiting without inventing a reduction in token work |
| Prefix reuse | Cache readiness and warm-up precede a hit | Reuse document context for a follow-up request |
| Execution rates | Same token work at different declared rates | Deliver the same answer with less modeled work |
| Four-condition composition | Separate policy and runtime interventions on one cohort | Inspect whether quality and execution gains compose |

The £120 discount/shipping example is an explanatory analogy, not captured Qwen output. The 6×6 matrices and four-candidate probabilities are exact small teaching calculations. They do not generate the separate illustrative 96-task result cohort, and do not constitute a trained language model.

LoRA follows the frozen-base / low-rank-update construction in the [original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685). The relative-reward film illustrates the direction of a group-relative update described in [TRL's GRPO documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/grpo_trainer); its normalized exponential tilt is explicitly not the full clipped GRPO objective.

## Figure story grammar

Free exploration keeps the original six views. Visitors can explicitly start a figure story: five inference chapters or seven training chapters. Each chapter has three phases:

1. Reveal the changed selection on the figure (3.2 s).
2. Pull focus into two explanatory lenses: inside the system / for the person asking (8 s).
3. Return to the figure and compare the consequence (4.2 s).

Hover or keyboard focus holds the explanatory card. Return/Escape restores the chart with focus on its playback control. A chosen result point exits into the corresponding free-exploration policy and budget; task selection is also retained. Replay is finite, visibility-aware, and stops in background tabs. Reduced motion uses manual chapters plus an always-readable explanation, with no automatic focus pulls.

Playback counts active viewing time. Pause, diagram inspection, offscreen holds and background tabs preserve the remaining scene duration, rather than restarting it. The chapter progress line shares those holds. Replay resets the clock, progress line and policy film even if the first chapter is already playing. A manually paused final chapter remains resumable; completion is shown only after its full viewing interval. Escape inside the native evidence dialog closes that dialog and leaves the underlying story paused.

The scaling marker follows actual cost coordinates; it does not treat 1×, 2×, 4× and 8× as equally spaced costs. The inference replay explicitly identifies the shared-prefix workload change and the combined reuse/rate effects. The mechanism story remains the place to isolate individual runtime interventions.
