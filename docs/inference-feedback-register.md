## Numerical and narrative boundaries

The two prefix lanes compare **service**, starting at the same zero. They do not claim that the requests originally entered the cohort's queues together. The prefix was computed by a prior request and was ready before reuse; that cold cost remains in the cohort. Prefill changes, decode duration and output count do not. A no-hit request must show equal lanes. The replay is illustrative, not physical GPU telemetry.

For execution, the current thousand-request view has two serial workers, not a thousand GPUs. Completion advantage may briefly plateau or shrink when a baseline request finishes; a constant per-request work advantage does not imply a monotonically widening completion-count lead at every instant. A new arrival/routing scene must preserve those distinctions. Changing numbers solely to produce an impressive curve is rejected.
