# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leaders, research leads, and technical peers evaluating Yashwant Bhyri for AI systems, agent engineering, AI application, and full-stack AI/ML roles. Most visitors will scan quickly first, then inspect one or two mechanisms in depth before deciding whether to open the résumé, GitHub, or a contact channel.

## Product Purpose

Present a company-agnostic, evidence-led portfolio that makes Yashwant's technical identity, strongest systems, personal contribution, and contact paths clear within seconds while rewarding deeper exploration. Success means a visitor can identify what each system does, what Yashwant built, how it behaves at runtime, and why the work matters without decoding internal project language.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, and technically assured. The experience should feel like a live systems observatory: complex mechanisms are visible and active, but the presentation remains calm enough for a recruiter to understand at a glance. Confidence comes from concrete architecture, behavior, and outcomes rather than generic superlatives.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS landing page assembled from interchangeable rounded cards.
- A videogame HUD that requires a controller, map, or dense navigation chrome.
- A dashboard that exposes every metric and label at the same visual priority.
- A static résumé grid that hides mechanisms behind paragraphs.
- An internal engineering document full of unexplained runtime terminology.
- A stripped-back redesign that removes the operational graphics or their motion.
- An autoplay experience that takes control away from the visitor.

## Design Principles

1. **Scroll is the controller.** Natural wheel, trackpad, touch, and keyboard scrolling should resolve to one complete presentation page at a time without adding game-like navigation.
2. **One focal state per viewport.** Every page should make one idea dominant; introductory copy should compact after it has been understood.
3. **Mechanisms explain the claim.** Operational graphics, highlighted annotations, and visible state changes should carry the technical story before supporting prose.
4. **Description before codename.** Human-readable project categories lead; Antigravity, MindScape, webGLR, and COL-VEO remain secondary identifiers.
5. **Evidence stays attached.** Contributions, runtime behavior, outputs, and impact must appear beside the mechanism or in the final microscope conclusion.
6. **Motion serves comprehension.** The automatic walkthrough remains active by default, but pause, replay, and direct exploration stay available.
7. **Contact is never a hunt.** The hero, persistent contact access, and closing page expose verified channels without repeating a large contact block on every page.

## Narrative Architecture

1. **Cinematic introduction.** The hero begins as one large central typed statement. Each statement is visibly backspaced; only after all three resolve does the presentation collapse into the upper-left credential capsule. CUHK-Shenzhen is identified with its crest and highlighted independently from the adjacent QS World #18 proof, while the experience counts receive their own evidence treatment.
2. **Quadrant reveal.** The selected-work deck enters on the right, followed by the lower-left contact surface. These elements do not reserve visual space before their reveal.
3. **Capability-first role lens.** Three target roles recompose the same 20 production skills across five capability domains: agent runtime, retrieval and memory, evaluation and control, performance and routing, and delivery and product. Each role closes with three concise examples of work already built; project names are provenance, not the organizing structure.
4. **Six-chapter index.** Five complete project walkthroughs cover **Real-time AI-Native Interviewing Platform**, **End-to-End Multimodal AI Production System**, **Medical AI Clinician-Support Product**, **LLM Serving, GPU Acceleration & Runtime Efficiency**, and **Reasoning, Alignment & Test-Time Scaling**. The sixth chapter is **Open-Source, Research & R&D Experience**.
5. **Two-stage flagship chapters.** Each flagship retains a full high-level architecture page, a personal-contribution tile, and a separate component microscope. The original three projects retain their one-time native smooth handoff. The richer compute introduction films stay inspectable until the visitor chooses a drilldown or scrolls onward. Every microscope stage carries a compact typed annotation and maturity label.
6. **Dynamic conclusion.** Conclusion is the final microscope stage, not a separate page. Product, contribution, runtime, output, impact, and reliability take center stage one at a time.
7. **Explicit evaluation loops.** Antigravity contains nine technical stages, ending with offline agent evaluation and versioned route-policy refinement. Filmora contains eight stages, ending with trace-led evaluation and refinement. MindScape contains eight stages, ending with a governed, offline clinical refinement loop after clinician review.
8. **Two-page research sequence.** Research records 1–3 occupy the first complete page and records 4–6 occupy the second. One record is spotlighted at a time; autoplay advances in order, scrolls to the continuation after record 3, holds on record 6, and then hands off to the closing contact page. Pause and direct card exploration remain available.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, touch-friendly targets, and complete silent usability. Default motion and operational animation are part of the experience and must remain intact. Visitors who explicitly request reduced motion receive stable end states without losing information. No content is available only through animation, color, hover, or audio.
